      How the war is fought

      1. Deploy. Each side hides a code of four different digits. Nobody else ever sees yours.
      2. Fire. Take turns firing a guess at the enemy's code.
      3. Dead is a right digit in the right place. Injured is a right digit in the wrong place. You learn how many, never which.
      4. Crack it. Four dead cracks the code. If the side that fired first cracks it, the other side gets one last stand to draw level.

      Example

      Their code 4071 Your guess 1024

      1 dead (the 0) and 2 injured (the 1 and the 4).

      Supplies

      Win the rock, paper, scissors draw for three crates, lose it for two and the first shot. Spend them from the Supplies tab, one crate a turn:

      • Recon flies a plane over their trench: a green flare if a digit is in their code, red if not.
      • Sniper takes a shot at one of their four soldiers: a hit proves a digit sits in his spot.
      • Smoke hides your squad: their next volley only reports total hits.

      Keys: digits to aim, Backspace to clear, Enter to fire. Long press a key, or turn on the pencil, to note digits you have ruled in or out. The Log tab keeps every volley, one side at a time or both to compare.
