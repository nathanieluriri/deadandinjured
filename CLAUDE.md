## Redesign: planned, not built

- The plan is `web/design/redesign-requests.md`. Read all of it before any work on the game's interface, title screen, end screens or match rules. Build nothing from it until the owner says go, then follow its build plan in order, starting with phase 0.
- The reference screenshots in `web/design/references/` are kept in the repository on purpose, at the owner's request, so they can be analyzed properly. Open and study every image itself, at full size, alongside its description, before designing or building. Never delete, move, compress or replace them. The folder's `README.md` explains how to study them.
